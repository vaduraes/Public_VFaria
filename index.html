Research and practice

At Duke Energy, I develop methods for generation dispatch and fuel management. Previously, I worked on capacity expansion and reliability at NREL, and on energy investment and infrastructure risk during my Ph.D. at NC State.

Across those roles, I’ve worked on short-term operations, long-term investment, and the resilience of infrastructure under extreme weather. I use mathematical optimization to compare feasible choices and trade-offs, alongside statistics and machine learning to forecast uncertain conditions, generate scenarios, and assess risk. That work includes energy storage and capacity expansion, offshore renewable portfolios, streamflow forecasting, marine device damage, and routing through flooded roads.
