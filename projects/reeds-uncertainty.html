Uncertainty in electricity investment decisions

Energy planning · Optimization · Related presentation: September 22, 2026

Capacity expansion models explore how an electricity system could evolve. This work uses Monte Carlo workflows to examine how uncertain technology costs and fuel prices affect investment decisions in the ReEDS model.

Considering multiple uncertain inputs helps reveal a range of possible investment outcomes instead of relying on a single set of cost assumptions. The analysis uses the geographic and operational detail of ReEDS to connect those inputs with generation choices across the U.S. electricity system. Results are described in the related PMAPS conference paper.
