Energy planning under hurricane risk

Infrastructure risk · Related presentations: 2023 and 2024

Infrastructure investments shape how an energy system can respond to extreme events. This research develops a multistage stochastic extension of TEMOA that represents hurricane damage and infrastructure vulnerability in electricity planning.

Related presentations

2024. Energy Systems Capacity Expansion Planning Under the Risk of Hurricane Damage. NC State Graduate Student Research Symposium, April 3. Abstract book (PDF).

2023. Energy Systems Capacity Expansion Planning Under the Risk of Hurricane Damage. INFORMS Annual Meeting, October 15 to 18.

Both presentations were by Victor A. D. de Faria, with Anderson R. de Queiroz as coauthor.
