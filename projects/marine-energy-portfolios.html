Marine renewable energy portfolios

Renewable energy · Artificial intelligence (AI) · Optimization · Related publications: 2022, 2023, and 2026

Offshore energy investment involves several decisions at once: which resources to use, where to develop them, and how to combine technologies and device designs. This work connects those choices through portfolio optimization, with applications off the North Carolina coast.

The 2022 study balances electricity cost and generation variability across more than 5,000 candidate sites, then uses capacity expansion modeling to assess cost targets for deployment. The 2023 study adds generative neural networks and risk-averse stochastic optimization to address low-generation conditions. More recent work jointly selects sites, device designs, and sizes while accounting for the power that can be transmitted to shore.
