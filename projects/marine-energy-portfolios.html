Marine renewable energy portfolios

Renewable energy · Related publications: 2022, 2023, and 2026

Offshore energy investment involves several decisions at once: which resources to use, where to develop them, and how to combine technologies and device designs. This work connects those choices through portfolio optimization.

The research includes representing variability and correlations across renewable resources, generating scenarios, and using stochastic optimization to evaluate investment decisions under uncertainty.
