Energy storage in long term planning

Energy planning · Renewable energy · Optimization · Related publication: 2024

Storage links decisions across time: electricity generated in one hour can meet demand in another. This collaboration improves how TEMOA represents renewable availability, demand, and storage operation so that long-term investment decisions reflect more of that daily behavior.

The Italian case study compares temporal resolutions and alternative technology assumptions. In scenarios without low-carbon baseload generation, storage is essential to achieving full power-sector decarbonization by 2050. The analysis also explores how a model’s choice of time slices changes its estimates of storage deployment and use.
