Teaching & mentoring

I enjoy sharing the ideas behind my work and helping students connect engineering questions with mathematical models. My experience includes teaching assistance in electrical machines and research mentoring in energy systems and machine learning.

CV & experience Research projects Publications

Topics of interest

My teaching and mentoring interests include:

  • Optimization and operations research: model formulation, linear and integer programming, and computational solution methods.

  • Energy systems and electrical engineering: electrical machines, power-system planning, renewable energy, and storage.

  • Machine learning for engineering: forecasting, computer vision, and evaluating predictive models.

  • Decision making under uncertainty: stochastic programming, scenario analysis, and infrastructure risk.

I value clear explanations, practical examples, and careful interpretation of results. A useful exercise is to formulate a small model, implement it, and then change its data or assumptions to understand why the results differ. My research in electricity planning, streamflow forecasting, and renewable-energy portfolios provides examples for these discussions.

Research mentoring

I have mentored Ph.D. students on research projects in collaboration with faculty and other researchers.

Lucas Barros Scianni Morais · Short-term load forecasting

Advised Morais during the development of a paper on neural-network methods for day-ahead electricity demand forecasting. We discussed model choices, interpretation of the results, and the paper’s writing. The study compared multilayer perceptrons, LSTMs, and GRUs for Brazil’s Central-Southeast electricity market, and Morais is the first author of the resulting Applied Energy paper.

Related publication · Forecasting project

Yen-Hsi Chou · 2023–2025

Mentored Chou on transfer learning and convolutional neural networks for identifying solar photovoltaic systems in aerial imagery. Chou is the first author of our paper on model architecture, training choices, and prediction errors.

Related publication · Solar detection project

Rob Miller · 2023–2024

Mentored Miller on marine renewable energy portfolio optimization, including site selection and device sizing through NCROEP projects. Miller is a coauthor of our paper on selecting offshore devices and sites under transmission constraints.
